# Ode One
He is my Helper and Protector ([Psalm 53.6/54.4](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Psalms+54&version=RSV))[^1], and hath become my Salvation ([Psalm 117.14/118.14](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Psalms+118&version=RSV)). This is my God and I will glorify Him, the God of my fathers and I will exalt Him ([Exodus 15.2](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Exodus+15&version=RSV)), for gloriously has He been glorified ([Exodus 15.1](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Exodus+15&version=RSV))[^o1]. (repeat)

[^1]: Greek numbering/Hebrew numbering.
[^o1]: The Biblical Ode 1 is Exodus 15.1–18 (The Song of Moses or the Song of the Sea).

<span id="o1-1"></span>
(O1.1/1) Have mercy on me, O God, have mercy on me.
Where shall I begin to weep for the actions of my wretched life? ([2 Ezra/Hebrew Ezra 9.13–10.1](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Nehemiah+9&version=RSV), [Lamentations 1.2](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Lamentations+1&version=RSV)) What first-fruit shall I offer ([Exodus 23.19](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Exodus+23&version=RSV), [Numbers 18.12–13](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Numbers+18&version=RSV), [Deuteronomy 26.1–2](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Deuteronomy+26&version=RSV), [Proverbs 3.9](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Proverbs+3&version=RSV), [Sirach 35.10](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Sirach+35&version=RSV), among others), O Christ, in this my lamentation? But in Thy compassion grant me forgiveness of sins.

<span id="o1-2"></span>
(O1.2/2) Have mercy on me, O God, have mercy on me.
Come, wretched soul, with thy flesh to the Creator of all. Make confession to Him, and abstain henceforth from thy past brutishness ([Proverbs 30.2](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Proverbs+30&version=RSV)); and offer to God tears of repentance ([2 Corinthians 7.10](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=2+Corinthians+7&version=RSV)).

<span id="o1-3"></span>
(O1.3/3) Have mercy on me, O God, have mercy on me.
I have rivaled in transgression Adam the first-formed man, and I have found myself stripped naked of God, of the eternal Kingdom and its joy, because of my sins ([Genesis 3.1–13](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Genesis+3&version=RSV)).

<span id="o1-4"></span>
(O1.4/4) Have mercy on me, O God, have mercy on me.
Woe to thee, miserable soul! How like thou art to the first Eve! For thou hast looked in wickedness and wast grievously wounded ([Micah 1.9](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Micah+1&version=RSV), [Nahum 3.19](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Nahum+3&version=RSV)); thou hast touched the tree and rashly tasted the deceptive food ([Genesis 3.1–13](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Genesis+3&version=RSV)).

<span id="o1-5"></span>
(O1.5/5) Have mercy on me, O God, have mercy on me.
Instead of the visible Eve, I have the Eve of the mind: the passionate thought in my flesh, ([Genesis 3.6](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Genesis+3&version=RSV), [1 John 2.16](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=1+John+2&version=RSV)) showing me what seems sweet; yet whenever I taste from it, I find it bitter ([Isaiah 5.20](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Isaiah+5&version=RSV), [Proverbs 27.7](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Proverbs+27&version=RSV)).

<span id="o1-6"></span>
(O1.6/6) Have mercy on me, O God, have mercy on me.
Adam was justly banished from Eden because he disobeyed one commandment of Thine, O Savior. What then shall I suffer, for I am always rejecting Thy words of life ([Genesis 3.23–24](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Genesis+3&version=RSV), [Acts 7.38](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Acts+7&version=RSV))?
 
<span id="o1-7"></span>
(O1.7/7) Have mercy on me, O God, have mercy on me.
By my own free choice have I incurred the guilt of Cain’s murder. I have killed my conscience, bringing the flesh to life and making war upon the soul by my wicked actions ([Genesis 4.8](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Genesis+4&version=RSV)).

<span id="o1-8"></span>
(O1.8/8) Have mercy on me, O God, have mercy on me.
O Jesus, I have not been like Abel in his righteousness ([Matthew 23.35](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Matthew+23&version=RSV)). Never have I offered Thee acceptable gifts or godly actions, a pure sacrifice or an unblemished life ([Genesis 4.4](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Genesis+4&version=RSV), [Hebrews 11.4](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Hebrews+11&version=RSV)).

<span id="o1-9"></span>
(O1.9/9) Have mercy on me, O God, have mercy on me.
Like Cain, O miserable soul, we too have offered, to the Creator of all, defiled actions and a polluted sacrifice and a worthless life: and so we also are condemned ([Genesis 4.5](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Genesis+4&version=RSV), [1 John 3.12](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=1+John+3&version=RSV)).

<span id="o1-10"></span>
(O1.10/10) Have mercy on me, O God, have mercy on me.
As the potter molds the clay  ([Genesis 2.7](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Genesis+2&version=RSV), [Jeremiah 18.1–10](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Jeremiah+18&version=RSV), [Romans 9.21](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Romans+9&version=RSV)), Thou hast fashioned me ([Psalm 138/139.13–16](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Psalms+139&version=RSV)), giving me flesh and bones ([Job 10.11](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Job+10&version=RSV)), breath and life ([Acts 17.25](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Acts+17&version=RSV)). But accept me in repentance, O my Maker and Deliverer and Judge.

<span id="o1-11"></span>
(O1.11/11) Have mercy on me, O God, have mercy on me.
I confess to Thee, O Savior, the sins I have committed ([James 5.16](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=James+5&version=RSV), [1 John 1.9](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=1+John+1&version=RSV)), the wounds of my soul and body, which murderous thoughts, like thieves, have inflicted inwardly upon me ([Luke 10.30](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Luke+10&version=RSV)).

<span id="o1-12"></span>
(O1.12/12) Have mercy on me, O God, have mercy on me.
Though I have sinned, O Savior, yet I know that Thou art full of loving-kindness ([Titus 3.4–5](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Titus+3&version=RSV)). Thou dost chastise with mercy and art fervent in compassion ([Psalm 50.3/51.1](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Psalms+51&version=RSV)). Thou dost see me weeping and dost run to meet me, like the Father calling back the Prodigal Son ([Luke 15.20](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Luke+15&version=RSV)).

<span id="o1-13"></span>
(O1.13/13) Have mercy on me, O God, have mercy on me.
I lie as an outcast before Thy gate ([Genesis 3.23–24](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Genesis+3&version=RSV), [Luke 16.20](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Luke+16&version=RSV)), O Savior. In my old age cast me not down empty into hell ([Psalm 54.16/55.15](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Psalms+55&version=RSV), [Psalm 70/71.9](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Psalms+71&version=RSV)); but, before the end comes, in Thy love grant me remission of sins ([Matthew 26.28](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Matthew+26&version=RSV), [Mark 1.4](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Mark+1&version=RSV), [Luke 1.77](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Luke+1&version=RSV), [Acts 2.38](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Acts+2&version=RSV), [Romans 3.25](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Romans+3&version=RSV)).

<span id="o1-14"></span>
(O1.14/14) Have mercy on me, O God, have mercy on me.
I am the man who fell among thieves ([Luke 10.30](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Luke+10&version=RSV)), even my own thoughts; they have covered all my body with wounds, and I lie beaten and bruised. But come to me, O Christ my Savior, and heal me ([Luke 10.34](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Luke+10&version=RSV)).

<span id="o1-15"></span>
(O1.15/15) Have mercy on me, O God, have mercy on me.
The Priest saw me first, but passed by on the other side; the Levite looked on me in my distress but despised my nakedness ([Luke 10.31–32](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Luke+10&version=RSV)). O Jesus, sprung from Mary, do Thou come to me and take pity on me ([Luke 10.33](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Luke+10&version=RSV)).

<span id="o1-16"></span>
(O1.16/16) Have mercy on me, O God, have mercy on me.
O Lamb of God, that takest away the sins of all ([John 1.29](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=John+1&version=RSV)), take from me the heavy yoke of sin ([Leviticus 26.13](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Leviticus+26&version=RSV), [Jeremiah 2.20](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Jeremiah+2&version=RSV), 35/28.2, [Hosea 11.4](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Hosea+11&version=RSV), [Matthew 11.29–30](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Matthew+11&version=RSV), [Galatians 5.1](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Galatians+5&version=RSV)), and in Thy compassion give me tears of compunction ([Psalm 6.7/6](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Psalms+6&version=RSV)).

<span id="o1-17"></span>
(O1.17/17) Have mercy on me, O God, have mercy on me.
It is time for repentance ([1 Corinthians 6.2](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=1+Corinthians+6&version=RSV)): to Thee I come, my Creator. Take from me the heavy yoke of sin, and in Thy compassion give me tears of compunction.

<span id="o1-18"></span>
(O1.18/18) Have mercy on me, O God, have mercy on me.
Reject me not, O Savior; cast me not away from Thy presence ([Psalm 50.13/51.11](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Psalms+51&version=RSV)). Take from me the heavy yoke of sin and in Thy compassion grant me remission of sins.

<span id="o1-19"></span>
(O1.19/19) Have mercy on me, O God, have mercy on me.
All mine offenses, voluntary and involuntary, manifest and hidden ([Psalm 89/90.8](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Psalms+90&version=RSV), [1 Corinthians 4.5](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=1+Corinthians+4&version=RSV)), known and unknown, do Thou forgive, O Savior, for Thou art God; be merciful and save me.

<span id="o1-20"></span>
(O1.20/20) Have mercy on me, O God, have mercy on me.
From my youth ([Psalm 128/129.1–2](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Psalms+129&version=RSV), inversion of several Scriptures that speak being faithful from youth, [Psalm 70/71.5](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Psalms+71&version=RSV) & 17, [Jeremiah 1.5](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Jeremiah+1&version=RSV), [Matthew 19.20](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Matthew+19&version=RSV)), O Savior, I have rejected Thy commandments ([2 Chronicles 33.1–2](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=2+Chronicles+33&version=RSV), [Prayer of Manasseh 10](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Prayer+of+Manasseh&version=RSV)). Ruled by the passions, I have passed my whole life in heedlessness and sloth ([Ecclesiastes 10.18](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Ecclesiastes+10&version=RSV), [Luke 15.13](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Luke+15&version=RSV)). Therefore, I cry to Thee, O Savior, even now at the end: Save me.

<span id="o1-21"></span>
(O1.21/21) Have mercy on me, O God, have mercy on me.
As the Prodigal, O Savior, I have wasted the substance of my soul in riotous living ([Luke 15.13](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Luke+15&version=RSV)), and I am barren of the virtues of holiness ([4 Ezra 5.1](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=2+Esdras+5&version=RSV))[^2]. In my hunger I cry: O giver of mercy, come quickly out to meet me and take pity on me.

[^2]: *The Apocalypse of Ezra* is an appendix in some Slavonic Bibles. 

<span id="o1-22"></span>
(O1.22/22) Have mercy on me, O God, have mercy on me.
I fall down, Jesus, at Thy feet ([Luke 8.47](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Luke+8&version=RSV)): I have sinned against Thee, be merciful to me. Take from me the heavy yoke of sin, and in Thy compassion, O God, accept me in repentance.

<span id="o1-23"></span>
(O1.23/23) Have mercy on me, O God, have mercy on me.
Enter not into judgment with me ([Psalm 142/143.2](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Psalms+143&version=RSV)), bringing before me the things I should have done, examining my words and correcting my impulses. But in Thy mercy overlook my sins and save me, O Lord Almighty.

*For Ss. Mary And Andrew In Tone Six*
Holy Mother Mary, pray to God for us.
Grant me the light of grace, from God’s providence on high, so that I may flee from the darkness of the passions and sing fervently the joyful tale of thy life, O Mary.

Holy Mother Mary, pray to God for us.
Bowing before the divine laws of Christ, thou hast drawn near to Him, forsaking the unbridled longings of sensual pleasure; and in the fear of God thou hast gained all the virtues as if they were one.

Holy Father Andrew, pray to God for us.
Through thine intercessions, O Andrew, deliver us from shameful passions and, we pray thee, make us now partakers of Christ’s Kingdom; for with faith and love we sing thy praises. 

Glory to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit.
Trinity beyond all being, worshipped in Unity, take from me the heavy yoke of sin, and in Thy compassion grant me tears of compunction.

Both now and ever, and unto ages of ages. Amen.
O Theotokos, the hope and protection of those who sing thy praises, take from me the heavy yoke of sin and, pure Lady, accept me in repentance.

# Ode Two
Attend, O Heaven, and I shall speak and sing ([Deuteronomy 32.1](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Deuteronomy+32&version=RSV)) in praise of Christ Who took flesh from a Virgin ([Hebrews 2.14](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Hebrews+2&version=RSV)) and came to dwell among us ([John 1.14](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=John+1&version=RSV))[^o2]. (repeat)

[^o2]: The Biblical Ode 2 is Deuteronomy 32.1–43 (The Second Song of Moses).

<span id="o2-1"></span>
(O2.1/24) Have mercy on me, O God, have mercy on me.
Attend, O Heaven, and I shall speak; give ear, O earth, to the voice of one who repents before God and sings His praise ([Deuteronomy 32.1](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Deuteronomy+32&version=RSV)).

<span id="o2-2"></span>
(O2.2/25) Have mercy on me, O God, have mercy on me.
Look upon me, God my Savior, with Thy merciful eye ([Tobit 3.3](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Tobit+3&version=RSV), 13.6, inversion of [Job 21.5](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Job+21&version=RSV)), and accept my fervent confession.

<span id="o2-3"></span>
(O2.3/26) Have mercy on me, O God, have mercy on me.
More than all men I have sinned ([1 Timothy 1.15](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=1+Timothy+1&version=RSV)); I alone have sinned against Thee ([Psalm 50.6/51.4](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Psalms+51&version=RSV)). But as God take pity on Thy creation, O Savior.

<span id="o2-4"></span>
(O2.4/27) Have mercy on me, O God, have mercy on me.
I am surrounded by the storm of sin, O compassionate Lord. But stretch out Thine hand to me, as once thou hast to Peter ([Matthew 14.29–31](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Matthew+14&version=RSV)).

<span id="o2-5"></span>
(O2.5/28) Have mercy on me, O God, have mercy on me.
I offer to Thee, O merciful Lord, the tears of the Harlot ([Luke 7.37–38](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Luke+7&version=RSV)). Take pity on me, O Savior, in Thy compassion.

<span id="o2-6"></span>
(O2.6/29) Have mercy on me, O God, have mercy on me.
With the lusts of passion ([Sirach 23.6](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Sirach+23&version=RSV), [1 Thessalonians 4.5](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=1+Thessalonians+4&version=RSV), [2 Peter 2.2](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=2+Peter+2&version=RSV), 12–16, [Jude 18](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Jude&version=RSV), [4 Maccabees 7.22](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=4+Maccabees+7&version=RSV), 13.2)[^3] I have darkened the beauty of my soul ([Romans 1.21](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Romans+1&version=RSV), [Ephesians 2.3](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Ephesians+2&version=RSV), 4.18), and turned my whole mind entirely into dust ([Genesis 2.7](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Genesis+2&version=RSV), 3.19, [4 Ezra 7.62–63](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=2+Esdras+7&version=RSV)).

[^3]: 4 Maccabees is an appendix in many Greek Bibles. It is a treatise on the woman with 7 sons martyred in the Maccabean revolt. The whole work argues for godly reason subduing the passions and the benefits that come from this.

<span id="o2-7"></span>
(O2.7/30) Have mercy on me, O God, have mercy on me.
I have torn the first garment that the Creator wove for me in the beginning ([Genesis 2.25](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Genesis+2&version=RSV)), and now I lie naked ([Genesis 3.7](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Genesis+3&version=RSV)).

<span id="o2-8"></span>
(O2.8/31) Have mercy on me, O God, have mercy on me.
I have clothed myself in the torn coat that the serpent wove for me by his counsel, and I am ashamed ([Genesis 3.5](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Genesis+3&version=RSV)).

<span id="o2-9"></span>
(O2.9/32) Have mercy on me, O God, have mercy on me.
I looked upon the beauty of the tree and my mind was deceived, and now I lie naked and ashamed ([Genesis 3.10](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Genesis+3&version=RSV)).

<span id="o2-10"></span>
(O2.10/33) Have mercy on me, O God, have mercy on me.
All the ruling passions have ploughed upon my back, making long furrows of wickedness ([Psalm 128/129.3](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Psalms+129&version=RSV), [Isaiah 51.23](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Isaiah+51&version=RSV)).

<span id="o2-11"></span>
(O2.11/34) Have mercy on me, O God, have mercy on me.
I have lost the beauty and glory with which I was first created; and now I lie naked and ashamed ([Genesis 3](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Genesis+3&version=RSV), [Apocalypse 16.15](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Revelation+16&version=RSV)).

<span id="o2-12"></span>
(O2.12/35) Have mercy on me, O God, have mercy on me.
Sin has stripped me of the robe that God once wove for me, and it has sewed for me garments of skin ([Genesis 3.20](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Genesis+3&version=RSV), [John 1.14](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=John+1&version=RSV)).

<span id="o2-13"></span>
(O2.13/36) Have mercy on me, O God, have mercy on me.
I am clothed with the raiment of shame as with fig leaves ([Genesis 3.7](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Genesis+3&version=RSV)), in condemnation of my self-willed passions ([Genesis 3.6](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Genesis+3&version=RSV), [1 John 2.16](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=1+John+2&version=RSV)).

<span id="o2-14"></span>
(O2.14/37) Have mercy on me, O God, have mercy on me.
I am clad in a garment that is defiled and shamefully bloodstained by a life of passion and self-indulgence ([Isaiah 64.6](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Isaiah+64&version=RSV), [Matthew 22.11–12](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Matthew+22&version=RSV)).

<span id="o2-15"></span>
(O2.15/38) Have mercy on me, O God, have mercy on me.
I have stained the garment of my flesh, O Savior, and defiled that which was made in Thine image and likeness ([Genesis 1.26–27](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Genesis+1&version=RSV)).

<span id="o2-16"></span>
(O2.16/39) Have mercy on me, O God, have mercy on me.
I have fallen beneath the painful burden of the passions ([Exodus 6.6–7](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Exodus+6&version=RSV), 23.5, [2 Ezra 15.15/Nehemiah](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Nehemiah+15&version=RSV) 5.15, [Psalm 37.5/38.4](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Psalms+38&version=RSV), [2 Corinthians 5.4](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=2+Corinthians+5&version=RSV)) and the corruption of material things; and I am hard pressed by the enemy ([Genesis 19.9](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Genesis+19&version=RSV), [1 Kingdoms/1 Samuel 13.6](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=1+Samuel+13&version=RSV)).

<span id="o2-17"></span>
(O2.17/40) Have mercy on me, O God, have mercy on me.
Instead of freedom from possessions ([Galatians 5.13](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Galatians+5&version=RSV), [1 Peter 2.16–19](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=1+Peter+2&version=RSV)), O Savior, I have pursued a life in love with material things ([Matthew 19.21–22](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Matthew+19&version=RSV), [Mark 10.21–22](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Mark+10&version=RSV), [Luke 12.13–21](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Luke+12&version=RSV)), and now I wear a heavy yoke ([Galatians 5.1](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Galatians+5&version=RSV)).

<span id="o2-18"></span>
(O2.18/41) Have mercy on me, O God, have mercy on me.
I have adorned the idol of my flesh with a many-colored coat of shameful thoughts (a subversion of [Genesis 37.3](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Genesis+37&version=RSV), [Epistle of Jeremiah 10](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Epistle+of+Jeremiah&version=RSV)), and I am condemned.

<span id="o2-19"></span>
(O2.19/42) Have mercy on me, O God, have mercy on me.

*The Synaxarion*
On the fifth Thursday of the Great Fast, we sing, according to the ancient tradition, the order of the Great Canon of Repentance.

Verses
Verily, Jesus, will I now sing unto Thee the Great Canon;
Grant Thou me tears of contrition and awe.

This reverential and compunctionate canon is “great” not only because of the large number of troparia, but because of the mystical thoughts, insights and pious references it contains. Andrew, bishop of Crete, composed the Great Canon of Repentance in the seventh century. He artistically included the entire history of the Old and New Testaments into one sacred song in order to urge all souls to perform good deeds and to shun evil ones, thus returning to God through repentance.
By the intercessions of Saint Andrew of Crete, O Christ God, have mercy upon us. Amen.

*Apolytikion Of St. Mary Of Egypt In Tone Eight*
In thee the image was preserved with exactness, O Mother; for taking up thy cross, thou didst follow Christ, and by thy deeds thou didst teach us to overlook the flesh, for it passeth away, but to attend to the soul since it is immortal. *Apolytikion Of St. Zosimas Of Palestine In Tone Eight*
The barren wilderness thou didst make fertile with the streams of thy tears; and by thy deep sighing thou hast given fruit through thy struggles a hundred-fold. Accordingly, thou hast become a star for the universe, sparkling with miracles. Therefore, O righteous Father Zosimas, intercede with Christ God to save our souls.

*Apolytikion Of St. Sophronios Of Jerusalem In Tone Four*
Thy work of justice did show thee to thy congregation as a canon of faith, the likeness of humility and teacher of abstinence, O Father and Patriarch Sophronios. Wherefore, by humility thou didst achieve exaltation, and by thy meekness richness. Intercede, therefore, with Christ to save our souls.

*Apolytikion For St. Andrew Of Crete In Tone Eight*
Thou hast shown thyself, O God-inspired Andrew of Crete, as a guide to the Orthodox Faith, a teacher of true worship and purity, O star of the universe and companion of the bishops, O wise one. Through thy light thou hast enlightened all, O harp of the Spirit. Therefore, intercede with Christ God to save our souls.
