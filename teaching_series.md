## About
This page lists the series Subdeacon Methodius (Loren) and Christopher have given for Holy Resurrection Orthodox Church in Tucson. About four series are done each year and each series is two (or three) weeks long, consisting of an informal discussion between the two with audience participate. (A bit like a live podcast with two hosts.)
